Rift Moments Clip Agent

Clip Agent is a desktop tool that turns League of Legends stream highlights into short captioned videos for the Rift Moments channels on YouTube and TikTok.

How it works

  1. It finds highlight moments in League of Legends streams and adds captions and a credit to the original creator.
  2. YouTube: finished clips are uploaded to the channel, and their view statistics are read back.
  3. TikTok: the owner connects their TikTok account with Log in with TikTok. The app then sends a finished clip to the TikTok inbox, where the owner opens the notification to edit and post it in the TikTok app.

It is used only by the owner of these channels and is not offered to anyone else.
