Privacy Policy

Rift Moments Clip Agent - last updated 10 September 2026

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses Google's YouTube APIs to upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's own YouTube channel, and to read the channel's video statistics and YouTube Analytics reports (views, likes, watch time and estimated revenue) for the videos it uploaded.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the Rift Moments channel. The app does not collect or process data about anyone else.

How data is stored and shared

The access token and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
