Rift Moments Clip Agent - last updated 11 September 2026

Clip Agent is a desktop tool run by the owner of the Rift Moments channels. It turns League of Legends stream highlights into short captioned videos that credit the original creator, and sends them to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

Use

The app is operated only by its owner for their own accounts. It is not offered as a service to other people. Connected accounts are used only for the actions described in the privacy policy.

Content

Every clip credits the original creator. Creators who want a clip removed can open an issue at the link below and it will be taken down.
